RB14 replay test guide

Record these as BeamNG replays (.rpl) and add them to the repo: tools/rb14/replay_analysis.py reads them.
For every recording
  • Stop for 3 s between tests - the analysis splits the replay at standstills.
  • Clean inputs: throttle fully on or off, brake fully on, steering held steady. Partial inputs blur the numbers.
  • Both directions for every cornering test (left, then right).
  • Hands off the steering on straights after a turn: centre it and leave it - a small correction hides the drift we are measuring.
  • Write down the car configuration and controller (wheel / pad / keyboard). Keep each replay to ~2-3 min (one replay per block below).
  • Big flat area (gridmap is fine); the longest straight you have for the top-speed runs (3 km+).

Replay A: straight-line tests

#What to doWhat it tells usDone
1Spawn and wait 10 s without touching anything.Spawns stably? Real wheel alignment at rest (it showed the 3.7° rear toe), ride height.
2Standing start, full throttle, steering centred, through the gears until the speed stops rising.0-100/200/300 km/h, acceleration at each speed, real top speed, gear changes, traction control.
3Then lift off and coast - no brake, no throttle - down to ~100 km/h.Most useful part. Coast-down separates air drag (grows with speed²) from rolling / scrub losses (constant): pins down the ~3 kN of extra drag.
4Accelerate to ~300 km/h, then full brake to a stop, steering centred.Braking g at each speed, brake balance, lock-ups, pull under braking.
5Repeat 2 with DRS open.DRS gain at top speed.
6Hold a steady ~150 km/h for 10 s, steering centred.Does it track straight before any cornering? (reference for the drift)
